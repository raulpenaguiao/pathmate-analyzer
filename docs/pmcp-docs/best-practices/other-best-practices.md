## Objectives & KPIs
https://my.pathmate.app/pmcp-documentation/doc-6-0/best-practices/objectives-kpis
Objective → at least one KPI → one concrete measure per KPI. Example table: skills, behaviour (10,000 steps), health (−10 mmHg systolic), engagement rate 70% (answering at least one question per week), app rating ≥ 4.0, retention ≥ 30% at 6 months, completion ≥ 40% in year 1.

## User Onboarding (app screens, not chat)
https://my.pathmate.app/pmcp-documentation/doc-6-0/best-practices/onboarding
One topic per screen, no scrolling, progress indicators, a go-back function. Typical steps: welcome, app preview, legal consent, registration (email or QR), personal data (only what's essential), push consent. Pitfalls: more than 10 screens, overloaded screens, asking for personal data too early.
