# Designing a Coaching Intervention

Source: https://my.pathmate.app/pmcp-documentation/doc-6-0/best-practices/designing-a-coaching-intervention · fetched 2026-09-29 · condensed extract

**Step 1: a "Sample Week".** List the interaction points, schedule them, specify each one's type (chat, questionnaire, video…) and define its trigger (time- and/or action-based). Table columns: Day | Time | Content | Type | Trigger.

**"Lazy user" scenario:** "Assume that some users may not respond to interaction requests from the app or coach." Plan how long users have to respond and what happens if they don't.

**Step 2: a comprehensive overview (master plan).** Catalogue every interaction point with its Timing | Conditions | Trigger | Type. Consider "exceptions", especially in the first days: "recurring 'check-in' or 'feedback' dialogues may not be relevant on the initial days or before users have completed specific tasks." Keep the KPIs measurable ([objectives-kpis](other-best-practices.md#objectives--kpis)).
