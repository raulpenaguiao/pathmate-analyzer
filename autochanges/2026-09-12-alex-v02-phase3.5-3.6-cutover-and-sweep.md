   spirometry measurement`, in **DAILY BASIS** (not PERIODIC) — targets the

## New finding: Rules-tab delete is cascade, not promote

Confirmed live (canary-tested on the already-inert Attic-gated chain before
touching anything active): **deleting a Rules-tab tree node deletes its
entire subtree** — the opposite of the Micro-Dialogs dialog-level delete
behavior found in Phase 2, where deleting a folder *promotes* its children
to the level above instead of removing them. These are two different delete
semantics in the same app, on different tree widgets — do not assume one
from the other. (This matters directly for any future rule-tree cleanup:
deleting a parent condition rule here is safe to also remove its whole
branch, unlike the dialog case where that same assumption caused a real
near-miss in Phase 2.)

## The cutover

Repointed Phase 3.3's sender rule (`Fire spirometry reminder...`)'s `Micro
dialog to start` from the original dialog to the new one. **One new
mechanic found**: since the new dialog is nested under the original (Phase
3.4's `New Dialog` behavior), it's listed in the `Micro dialog to start`
dropdown as a **path**, not a bare name —
`"Prompt patient to conduct daily spirometry > Prompt patient to conduct
daily spirometry (v02)"` — found via the same next-page pagination search
already used for other dialog-picker fields, on page 2 of the list.
Confirmed via `parse_rule_fields()` before and after: only
`microDialogToStart` changed, `sendHourVariable` (`$spiroMesTime`),
`actions`, and the 4h timeout were all untouched by the edit.
